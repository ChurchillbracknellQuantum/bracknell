Bracknell Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Who uses Bracknell

Bracknell is a personal app with exactly one user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, Bracknell connects to the owner's own Gmail accounts in order to:

  • read messages the owner has received, so the owner can search them;
  • move messages to Gmail Trash, only after the owner has approved each list.

Bracknell does not read drafts, does not send email, and never deletes email permanently.

Where the data goes

Messages are stored only on the owner's own computer. The sign-in tokens are encrypted on that computer. Nothing is sent to a Bracknell server (there is none). Nothing is shared with, sold to or shown to anyone else. The data is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Bracknell's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Bracknell's access at any time in the Google Account settings, under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services". Deleting Bracknell's folder on the owner's computer deletes everything it stored.
